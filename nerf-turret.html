Two-Axis Nerf Turret

Hardware hackathon · 2nd of 40 teams

Situation. Our team had 24 hours to design, print, and assemble a two-axis Nerf turret.

Task. I developed the servo-driven indexer, focusing on rigidity, precision, and tolerances.

Action. I iterated several indexer designs during assembly. The turret combined full 360-degree rotation, a custom thrust bearing, gravity-fed loading, and Arduino/Raspberry Pi motor control.

Result. We placed second out of 40 teams, with high scores for complexity and completeness.
