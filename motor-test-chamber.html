Motor Life Test Chamber

Internship

An eight-motor endurance rig needed faster turnaround and reliable power and temperature control.

I improved motor cooling, built the power distribution box, automated the AC, and streamlined the operator workflow.

I integrated air channels into the printed mounts, wired nine supplies totaling roughly 15 kW, and added Arduino-based IR control with tuned PID and compressor hysteresis. I also wrote an SOP and reorganized the bench around assembly jigs.

Cooling time halved, and overall cycle time fell from 10 minutes to four. At the time of the portfolio, the power box had completed 13,000+ three-minute throttle-blip cycles without shorts, wiring failures, or downtime.
