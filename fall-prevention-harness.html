Fall-Prevention Harness Concept

ASME six-hour CADathon · First place

Situation. A six-hour ASME CADathon challenged us to design wearable technology.

Task. A teammate and I developed a harness concept to support older adults while allowing movement around a room.

Action. We modeled configurable ceiling rails, a carrier with rotary mecanum wheels and accessible clips, and a dual-offset cycloidal gearbox integrated into a belt pulley. The concept would respond to sudden torque changes associated with a fall.

Result. We won first place. The deliverable was a CAD concept; fall-prevention performance has not been demonstrated by a tested device.
