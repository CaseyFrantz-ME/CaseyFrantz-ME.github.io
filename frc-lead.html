FIRST Robotics Competition, Mechanical Lead

2023 season

Situation. Our FRC team needed two reliable robots for the 2023 season, each with swerve drive, a telescoping arm, and a cube-and-cone intake.

Task. I led manufacturing and drove one robot in competition.

Action. I turned the design subteams' CAD into aluminum, polycarbonate, and printed parts using manual mills, lathes, and a CNC router, and helped keep the robot reliable through high-contact matches.

Result. The team reached the world championship, won engineering awards, and placed in the top 40 of roughly 3,000 teams.
